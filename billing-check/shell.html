This example calculates US$2.00 and compares it with US$1.50 invoiced: a US$0.50 difference. Use * for a default price or a customer ID for an override. A header-only invoice file is valid when no usage was invoiced.

What this release checks

Flat per-unit prices, customer-specific overrides, included monthly units, missing invoice lines, net credits and duplicate event/line IDs. Exact duplicate IDs count once. Conflicting duplicate IDs stop the comparison. Unpriced usage and invoice lines with no matching usage are marked for review and excluded from financial totals.

Results stay separate by customer, meter, month and currency. Amounts use exact decimal arithmetic and round half up to two decimal places once per group. Supported currencies: USD, NZD, AUD, EUR, GBP, CAD, CHF, SGD and HKD. Maximum 100,000 rows and 6 MB per file.

Does it connect directly to Stripe?

No. Export and map your customer usage and net invoice lines into the supplied CSV templates. Payment collection for this product uses Stripe; billing analysis itself never connects to your Stripe account.
