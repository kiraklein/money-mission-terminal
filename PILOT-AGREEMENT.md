# RunwayZero Early Design Partner Agreement — Draft

**Important:** This is a practical starter draft, not legal advice. Have New Zealand counsel review it before signing or taking payment.

This Early Design Partner Agreement (Agreement) is between **Klein Consulting Ltd** (Provider) and the customer identified in the signature block (Customer).

## 1. Pilot
Provider will give Customer access to an early version of RunwayZero and may provide setup, feedback sessions and reasonable product support during the pilot.

## 2. Pilot term
The pilot begins on the agreed start date and continues for 30 days unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

## 3. Fee
The pilot fee is **USD $250** for the 30-day term, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes and are non-refundable once the pilot begins.

## 4. Customer data
Customer should not provide confidential banking credentials or highly sensitive personal information. Provider will only use information supplied for the purpose of delivering and improving the pilot.

## 5. Feedback
Customer may provide product feedback. Provider may use that feedback without restriction, provided Provider does not publicly identify Customer without permission.

## 6. Confidentiality
Each party will protect non-public information received from the other and use it only for the pilot. This obligation does not apply to information that is public, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source.

## 7. Early-stage product
Customer acknowledges that RunwayZero is an early-stage product, may change materially and may contain errors. It is a planning aid only and is not accounting, financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

## 8. Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Provider's aggregate liability relating to the pilot is capped at the fees paid by Customer under this Agreement, except where liability cannot legally be limited.

## 9. Termination
Either party may terminate the pilot on 7 days' written notice. Sections intended to survive termination, including confidentiality, feedback rights and liability limits, continue after termination.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by New Zealand law and the parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the New Zealand courts.

## 11. Entire agreement
This Agreement and any written order form or email confirming the pilot and start date form the entire agreement for the pilot and may be amended only in writing.
