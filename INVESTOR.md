# RunwayZero — investor note

## One line
A privacy-first founder finance tool that starts with the fastest possible answer to “how long do we have?”

## Problem
Early-stage founders routinely manage runway in brittle spreadsheets or tools that are too heavy for the question they are trying to answer.

## Wedge
A zero-signup, zero-integration runway calculator that returns burn, runway, cash-out date and a growth-adjusted scenario in seconds.

## Expansion
1. Scenario comparison
2. Runway alerts
3. Shared investor / board views
4. Founder finance operating cadence
5. Optional accounting and banking integrations
6. Accelerator and VC portfolio dashboards

## Business model hypothesis
- Free: instant runway calculator
- Pro: founder workspace, scenarios and alerts
- Portfolio: accelerator / VC portfolio plan

## Current stage
MVP / design-partner stage. No revenue, user-growth or market-share claims are being made.

## What I am looking for
Design partners, pre-seed investors and introductions to founders who manage runway manually today.
