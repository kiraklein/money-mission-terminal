# The Exchange — prototype

A responsive, front-end concept for a global intelligence and social forecasting network.

## Run
Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or dependencies.

## Included
- Market discovery across business, technology, wealth, culture and power
- Search and category filters
- Illustrative community forecast probabilities
- Analyst profile and intelligence briefing panels
- Proposed Exchange Pro tier at USD $19/month
- Early-access and membership interaction prototypes

## Important product boundary
This prototype is **not** a real-money prediction market or securities exchange. All market probabilities, profiles, metrics and changes are illustrative demo data. No orders, deposits, withdrawals, payments, user accounts or live market feeds are implemented. Do not represent it as a live exchange.

Before enabling real-money event contracts, obtain jurisdiction-specific legal advice and required authorisations. Build identity, sanctions/AML, age and location controls, market surveillance, resolution governance, custody/payment controls and audited security before any regulated launch.

## Commercial hypothesis
Free network; Exchange Pro proposed at USD $19/month; institutional intelligence and data licensing priced by contract. Pricing is a hypothesis to test, not a validated offer.
